Practical notes

  • SIM: buy a tourist eSIM before flying (AIS or dtac, ~THB 300–500 for 8–15 days unlimited).
  • Cash: island tours, longtails, songthaews, street food and park fees are cash-only. ATM fee is THB 220 per withdrawal — withdraw big. Carry THB 8,000–10,000.
  • Grab works in Bangkok and Phuket; patchy in Ao Nang (songthaews / hotel cars).
  • Temple dress: shoulders and knees covered for Grand Palace, Wat Phra Kaew, Wat Pho. Pack long trousers + light scarf each.
  • Weather: Phuket/Krabi is monsoon-to-high-season transition — short heavy showers, keep island days flexible. Bangkok should be dry, 32–33°C.
  • Reconfirm Vietjet VZ345 24h before; check in online with VB4WTE.
  • Scams: anyone outside the Grand Palace saying it's closed is lying. Use metered taxis or Grab.
  • VAT refund: purchases over THB 2,000 from "VAT Refund for Tourists" shops — get P.P.10 stamped at customs before check-in at BKK, claim after immigration. +30 min.
  • Hotel phone: ibis Bangkok Sukhumvit 4 — +66 2 667 5888
